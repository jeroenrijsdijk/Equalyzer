about=Splits polygons three ways. Equal parts: draw a direction line and get N parts of exactly equal area, cut parallel to the line. Equal areas: the same with a target area per part. Parking bays: select a strip (or draw a line over it), and it is cut into 2.5 x 5 m bays, the orientation and count inferred from the strip's own size, with every attribute of the strip copied to the bays and the option to replace the strip in its layer. Works in QGIS 3 and QGIS 4, and in geographic as well as projected CRSs.

changelog=2.0.0
  Release of the fork. Since the original 1.4.5:
  - Runs on QGIS 4 (Qt6) as well as QGIS 3.16+ (Qt5); all version-dependent details sit in one compatibility block.
  - Equal-parts splitting rewritten: cut positions come from the polygon's exact cumulative-area profile (closed form), parts are made by intersecting with band polygons so they always add up to the original, and ellipsoidal measurement is corrected with local Newton steps. Tested against shapely (tests/).
  - New parking-bay mode: strip depth decides parallel (cut every 5 m) or perpendicular (every 2.5 m), the count follows from the length with a minimum bay size instead of plain rounding, orientation and sizes are configurable, and the dialog flags strips that do not fit.
  - Geographic CRSs are handled by measuring and cutting in the matching UTM zone; right angles in degrees are not right angles on the ground.
  - Draw a direction line with nothing selected and the polygon under it is picked.
  - Repeated splits of the same layer collect in one Split Parts layer; every source attribute is copied to the parts; the original can be deleted or replaced in its own layer through one undoable edit command.
  - Fixed: areas reported as 0.0000 (source CRS was set after the ellipsoid); absolute area thresholds that silently dropped parts in geographic CRSs; snapping setup that could break the direction tool.
  - Error handling: failures at the entry points are reported with a traceback in the log panel instead of being swallowed. Diagnostics can be enabled with the Equalyzer/debug setting.
